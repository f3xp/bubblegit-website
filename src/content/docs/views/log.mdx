<Term html={shot} />

## Commits

The left pane lists commits with a graph column. Commits load a page at a time. The next page is fetched a screenful before the cursor reaches the bottom, so scrolling does not stall. Side branches of merges are never dropped.

## Selected commit

The right pane shows the selected commit's message and its patch, highlighted the same way a working-tree diff is.

A merge shows what it brought in over its first parent. The initial commit shows its whole tree.

## Read-only

Nothing in this view writes to the repository. The staging and commit keys do nothing here.
