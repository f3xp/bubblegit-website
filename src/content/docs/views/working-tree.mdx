Press `1` to open the working tree. `tab` switches between the two panes.

<Term html={shot} />

## Files

The left pane lists changed files with git's two-letter status. The first letter is the staged column, the second the unstaged column. `?` marks an untracked file.

## Diff

The right pane shows the diff of the selected file, syntax highlighted. The pane title says whether you are looking at the worktree side or the staged side. Press `t` to toggle between them.

A binary file has no diff to show. The pane says `binary file, no textual diff` instead.

## Staging

`space` stages what is under the cursor and `a` stages the hunk around it. See [Staging and committing](/staging-and-committing/) for the full set of keys.
